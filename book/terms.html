Terms of Use

  • A booking is confirmed instantly and gets a booking number. Keep it with your mobile number to change or cancel.
  • Payment is at the venue before play (cash or card). The app never takes any money.
  • You can change or cancel in the app up to 2 hours before the start. After that, contact the venue directly.
  • Please arrive 10 minutes early. Play ends at the booked time.
  • Repeated no-shows without cancelling may block bookings from your number.
  • The venue may cancel or move a booking for an emergency or maintenance and will tell you.
  • At most 2 upcoming bookings per mobile number; bookings open one week ahead.
